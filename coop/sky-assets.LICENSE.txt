Rainline Camp sky assets

Evening Sky HDRI 049 A
Credit: ambientCG
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://ambientcg.com/a/EveningSkyHDRI049A
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

The game uses a 2048 x 512 sky hemisphere derived from the supplied
2K tone-mapped panorama. The ground and photographed sun were cropped
out. The last sky row extends to the horizon, where the shader fades
into the dynamic atmosphere.

The Milky Way panorama (eso0932a)
Credit: ESO/S. Brunier
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Original photo: art/sky/eso0932a.jpg. The game texture was resampled
to 4096 x 2048 and recompressed. The shader projects it onto the sky
and adjusts the visible brightness according to the time and weather.

CGI Moon Kit, lroc_color_2k.jpg
Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright (USRA)
and Noah Petro (NASA/GSFC).
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/

The 2048 x 1024 NASA color map is included unaltered in the project.
The shader samples it on the Moon and lights the surface by Sun direction.

Observer star atlas

The 5,044 positions, visible magnitudes and B−V color indices are from the
Extended Hipparcos Compilation (XHIP), Erik Anderson and Charles Francis,
via d3-celestial's stars.6.json (Olaf Frohn). The game reprojects and
rasterizes those measurements into a galactic-coordinate texture.
